# Judgement checks

These are the checks the `check_layout` tool doesn't automate yet, because they need data the blueprint lacks or a designer's eye. Apply every one, and record each as passed, an issue, or not applicable with the reason (for example, "Layered stronghold: not an assault"). The ids are SKILL.md's Layout Principles (LP-n) and the pacing checks below (PC-n). When a call is close, read the id's backing in [`PRINCIPLES.md`](PRINCIPLES.md).

## Mechanic and archetype

- **Mechanic fit:** the Mechanic has terrain to act on (an elevation hazard needs two or more tiers with paths between them). Abilities the mission grants have terrain that rewards them (LP-1).
- **Loadout reachability:** no required goal is reachable only by units or skills the player may not have yet. Each gate is reached after the Beat that teaches what passes it (LP-3).
- **Graph shape fits the archetype:** no "long hallway" (a pure chain with no branch, loop or place to settle) unless the archetype is deliberately linear (LP-14).

## Reading and guidance

- **Branch legibility:** every Zone with two or more forward exits has a cue for each exit, visible from the branch Zone (LP-10).
- **Guidance cues:** the route to each goal is marked by a directional line of repeated doodads, or by rising elevation. A single tall landmark isn't enough; a landmark needs colour or light contrast and repeated visibility along the route (LP-10).
- **Show, then withhold:** the final goal is glimpsed early (an opening pan or an overlook) and shown again closer as the Beats advance. A Zone that opens at a Beat is visible from a Zone opened earlier (LP-9).
- **Readability matches difficulty:** early or easy missions keep main threats out of line-of-sight blockers and unseen high ground (LP-4).

## Pacing checks

The pacing checks spell out LP-6 along the critical path and the mission's timeline.

- **PC-1 Callout spacing:** along the critical path, no two loud Zones (a large force, an objective, a hazard, a scripted attack) are adjacent without a quiet Zone between them.
- **PC-2 Refuge and prospect alternation:** no run of three or more prospects without a refuge. Refuge spacing in travel time suits the mission's tempo (LP-5).
- **PC-3 Prep before climax:** the Zone before each climax has no enemies, room to gather, and ideally a cache or reinforcements.
- **PC-4 Reward after peak:** each peak is followed by a reward (an expansion, a cache, reinforcements, an unlock, a story Transmission), and reward types vary (LP-6, LP-12).
- **PC-5 Start Zone:** intimate and defensible, and within a sensible travel time of the first conflict: neither immediate nor a chore (LP-11).
- **PC-6 Timeline:** wave cadence, Beats, bonus objectives and Transmissions don't pile up or leave dead time. SC2 pacing is partly temporal.

## Threats, risk and reward

- **Enemies are walls:** strong enemy groups sit across the line from entry to exit. Each big threat's Zone (wide or narrow) is chosen on purpose (LP-7).
- **Enemy strength:** the enemy matches Blizzard's sizes and every enemy base is a working base, as in SKILL.md's **Enemy bases**: about 120 units on Normal, guard groups on the ground between bases, a town hall with workers and gas in every base.
- **Bounce asymmetry:** in a Zone the player holds with two or more entrances, the defender crosses between entrances faster than the attacker.
- **Resource gradient:** the start has modest resources; richer resources are further out or exposed; nothing rich sits in a refuge.
- **Scoutable risk:** each optional reward Zone shows a scout its kind of defence and kind of reward (LP-10).
- **Side content cued:** optional objectives are one Connection off the critical path, with a sight line or cue. True secrets are rare (LP-12).
- **Layered stronghold** (assaults): the core is reached through at least two enemy-held Zones of non-falling threat (LP-8).

## Teaching

- **Demonstration:** each new hazard or enemy type is shown acting on units other than the player's, visible from the approach, before engagement (LP-3).
- **Tour:** the first Beat's route passes or overlooks many of the Zones later Beats use, or an opening pan covers them (LP-9).
- **Briefings in refuges:** long Transmission sequences play while the player is in a safe Zone (LP-11).

## Zone content

- **No pure open bowl:** large Zones have at least one sub-feature (an elevation change, a line-of-sight blocker, a choke, cover) unless their role is `arena` (LP-17).
- **Dressing matches function:** dangerous Zones don't look inviting, and refuges don't look hostile (LP-16).
