# Hero-mission Principles: backing

Each principle's backing. Source tags: [dev] a developer on their own work, [data] game files, [wiki] fan wiki, [forum] player posts, [critic] press, [ld] level-design theory, [secondary] an explainer drawing on developers, [mine] our inference. Talk citations are YouTube auto-captions, linked at their timestamp; names and numbers in them can be misheard. No Blizzard map file has been measured, so every number here is a starting point to check in the Editor.

## HM-1 Mechanic from the fantasy, then a chain of encounters

**Derive each mission's one dominant mechanic from the heroes' fantasy, then author the mission as a chain of named encounters.**

- *Backing:* Every Blizzard hero mission has one dominant mechanic: detection in Breakout, Dominate in Ghost of a Chance, one ability per room in Back in the Saddle, pack-leader gauntlets in Supreme [wiki]. Huck, then lead level designer, lists the pattern first-hand: walls of fire, flash freezes, day/night zombies, then LotV's moving base [dev]. Blizzard picks the mechanic from the character.
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced for mechanic-first and for the encounter as the unit. Our inference for "6–10 encounters per mission".
- *Sources:* [Fandom: Breakout](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Breakout), [Fandom: Back in the Saddle](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Back_in_the_Saddle), [LotV panel 11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKp_7buQ-v8&t=697s), [Huck, BlizzCon 2016 interview 04:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lylVQAwZhqM&t=249s), [LotV panel 32:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKp_7buQ-v8&t=1963s), [GoW level design talk 21:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=1307s), [Desperados III devcom 26:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=1590s), [Taylor Part 2](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/ten-principles-of-good-level-design-part-2-), Engadget's 2011 interview with Jaime Griesemer ("Half-minute Halo")

## HM-2 A small hub, visible forks, hidden linearity

**Use a small hub with 2–3 spokes, each forking visibly, make fight spaces interconnected, and hide the linearity.**

- *Backing:* Blizzard hero missions are linear chains of rooms or small hubs [wiki]. Mimimi's levels are linear up to a gate, then T-shaped, and the briefing names the branches. "If you have 20 paths but they're all obfuscated... they will think it's linear." The fork matters only because the player loses the branch not taken, while hidden extra routes stay unsignposted as replay rewards [dev]. "We never have one strict linear path; we always want options" [dev]. Mimimi's later fully open islands were "a beast to test" [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Where sources disagree:* Serr names "too linear" as the commonest failure [ld], and a long single hallway reads as a corridor rather than a space; both push toward openness. Serr concedes that cinematic, story-driven moments are hard in open maps.
- *Sources:* [Fandom: Conviction](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Conviction), [Shadow Tactics GDC 19:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1198s), [21:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1261s), [Desperados III devcom 42:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2535s), [GOG interview](https://www.gog.com/news/shadow_gambit_the_cursed_crew_is_out_read_an_interview_with_games_head_of_design_moritz_wagner), [Shadow Tactics postmortem](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mimimi-s-i-shadow-tactics-blades-of-the-shogun-i-), [Halo talk 35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=76kZtNaetx0&t=2145s), [Meaningful Choice 04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BEF4GVNzkUw&t=253s), [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/21592), [Non-linear levels 14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTBor4rhnQs&t=870s), [16:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTBor4rhnQs&t=994s)

## HM-3 Teach, combine, examine

**Teach each hero ability in its own encounter (prompt it, give a target built for it, practise it), then test it in combination and end each tool's arc in a "final exam".**

- *Backing:* Back in the Saddle introduces Kinetic Blast against a lone Marauder with a prompt, then Crushing Grip against the next squad [wiki]. Ghost of a Chance prompts "use Dominate" on a Siege Tank [wiki]. DoW2: Retribution's tutorial ends in a boss that "tests everything learned" [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Sources:* [Fandom](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Back_in_the_Saddle), [Fandom](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Ghost_of_a_Chance), [Darwin Yuen](https://darwinyuen-designer.squarespace.com/dow-2-retribution), [GoW level design talk 33:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=1996s), [50:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=3054s), [Hitman talk 11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=706s), [22:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=1331s), [Pillars retrospective 29:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0RW3upLoJI&t=1753s)

## HM-4 No two fights without a reason

**No two adjacent fight spaces without a reason. Fill the gaps with loot, lore, dialogue and recovery.**

- *Backing:* Obsidian audited Deadfire for fights per area and asked of adjacent fights whether both were needed: "Let the player breathe for a second" [dev]. Sawyer says the first Pillars had the same fault: "We just put too many fights in the game." A patch "thinned out the encounters" and strengthened the rest [dev]. Santa Monica flagged a second arena as too like the first and added a character "to spice it up" [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced (two first-party Obsidian accounts, one Santa Monica, plus theory).
- *Sources:* [Shacknews, Craddock excerpt](https://shacknews.com/article/103473/beneath-a-starless-sky-pillars-of-eternity-and-the-infinity-engine-era-of-rpgs?page=27), [Pillars retrospective 40:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0RW3upLoJI&t=2453s), [GoW level design talk 17:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=1059s), [Taylor Part 1](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-ten-principles-of-good-level-design-part-1-)

## HM-5 One mode shift, in the second half

**Change the mode once, in the second half, make sure the new mode carries real decisions, and budget rebuilds for it.**

- *Backing:* Blizzard hero missions almost always shift mode late: the tram defence in Back in the Saddle, the timed escape in Conviction, the bike chase in The Escape, the collapse escape in Evil Awoken [wiki]. Game Informer called the bike section "mechanical and rather boring" [critic]. The praised examples come from Chaos Rising: a holdout "against huge waves... and artillery barrages", and "a timed mad dash back to the starting point" through cleared ground to rescue a squad member [critic].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced as a pattern. Moderate on what makes a shift succeed (backtracking under pressure and holdouts are praised).
- *Where sources disagree:* Wagner says cinematic surprises helped kill the tactics genre: in a tactical game you want "to not be surprised" [dev]. Mode shifts should be telegraphed turns between encounters, never a surprise in the middle of one.
- *Sources:* [Fandom: Conviction](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Conviction), [Fandom: Evil Awoken](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Awoken), [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2016/03/30/nova-covert-ops-pt-1-impressions-fresh-approach-fading-fun), [GamesRadar Chaos Rising review](https://www.gamesradar.com/warhammer-40000-dawn-of-war-ii-chaos-rising-review/), [Huck NCO interview 08:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S1_Gwk7F7k4&t=505s), [Shadow Tactics GDC 05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=318s)

## HM-6 Urgency from the situation

**Create urgency through the situation (a pursuer, a collapse, a patrol cycle) and rewards placed ahead, not countdown timers. Allow a timer only if progress extends it or it serves a rescue the player cares about.**

- *Backing:* At least 5 posters in one thread hate timed missions [forum]. A TL author separates urgency the player cares about (Piercing the Shroud's hunting hybrid, Supernova's fire wall) from a timer the characters care about [forum]. NCO players enjoyed missions running "up to an hour due to no hard timer" [forum].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced for player dislike of countdowns and for situational pulses. Thin for the Old Hatreds note.
- *Where sources disagree:* Blizzard deliberately shipped Sky Shield's countdown, where repairing stabilisers "buys the player more time" [dev]. Chaos Rising's timed rescue dash was praised [critic].
- *Sources:* [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/21592), [TL](https://tl.net/forum/single-player/507931-favourite-campaign-missions), [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/11624), [warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Old_Hatreds), [Desperados III devcom 30:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=1845s), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/68627/interview-starcraft-2-heart-swarm), [LotV panel 11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKp_7buQ-v8&t=697s), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/warhammer-40000-dawn-of-war-ii-chaos-rising-review/)

## HM-7 A short critical path

**Keep the critical path short and let optional content stretch the mission for players who want it.**

- *Backing:* Supreme's achievement implies an intended run of under 12 minutes on Hard [wiki]. In the Enemy's Shadow took one player about 45 minutes the first time, while others speedran it in 3:29–3:37 [forum]. Obsidian shortened Deadfire's critical path and polished it more than off-path content [dev].
- *Evidence:* Thin for SC2 lengths (two anecdotes and one achievement). Mimimi's lengths are a different genre's norm.
- *Where sources disagree:* Short intended runs (Supreme) versus players enjoying hour-long exploratory NCO missions. Both are satisfied if length lives in optional content.
- *Sources:* [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/starcraftiiheartoftheswarm/supreme-achievements/z04bb8), [TL NCO p.5](https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/512079-nova-covert-ops-mission-pack-2-release-2016-aug-2?page=5), [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/103473/beneath-a-starless-sky-pillars-of-eternity-and-the-infinity-engine-era-of-rpgs?page=27), [Shadow Tactics GDC 16:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1008s), [33:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=2007s), [Desperados III devcom 03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=194s)

## HM-8 Escalate

**Escalate within each mission and across the campaign: start small and grow, with squad, ally competence and set-piece scale rising over time.**

- *Backing:* Relic's Retribution producer conceded that players missed "strategic escalation into bigger fights" in DoW2 [dev]. Producer Jeff Lydell calls "that escalation of warfare, where you start out small" a Relic value, and says starting large and "whittl[ing] it down... becomes less interesting over time" (said of multiplayer) [dev]. Whispers of Doom grows the force in steps [wiki].
- *Evidence:* Moderate (two Relic statements, one Santa Monica account, precedent).
- *Sources:* [Gaming Nexus](https://gamingnexus.com/Article/3052/Warhammer-402c000-Dawn-of-War-II---Retribution-Postmortem-Interview), [GamesRadar retrospective p.2](https://www.gamesradar.com/how-dawn-of-war-ii-risked-it-all-to-push-a-genre-forward/2/), [Fandom](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Whispers_of_Doom), [Atreus talk 40:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lbyGzzcKg9U&t=2420s), [AI and Games TLOU 31:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7MVzKuQ5SfY&t=1881s)

## HM-9 Plan across the campaign

**Plan pacing across the campaign with a per-mission sheet. Never repeat a setting or objective type back to back, introduce one high-impact feature at a time, and keep story climaxes mechanically familiar.**

- *Backing:* Shadow Tactics ran about 25 hours on three guard types and no levelling. Variety came from "high-impact features" (night, snow footprints) graphed per mission against expected excitement. Settings never repeat back to back, objective types are balanced (never three assassinations in a row), and the big story-climax missions add no new features [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced for the sheet and its rules (first-party Mimimi). Thin for the remove-a-tool lever (one review).
- *Sources:* [Shadow Tactics GDC 28:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1696s), [30:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1819s), [31:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1882s), [33:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=2007s), [17:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1071s), [GamesRadar Chaos Rising review](https://www.gamesradar.com/warhammer-40000-dawn-of-war-ii-chaos-rising-review/), [Huck, BlizzCon 2016 interview 14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lylVQAwZhqM&t=869s)

## HM-10 Cap power below solo the map

**Cap hero power below "solo the map", give hero mobility a real cost, and never let a hero cover a gap in the squad's roster.**

- *Backing:* This is the most common complaint in the forum pass. About 15 posters across 5 threads say Kerrigan made HotS trivial or the army irrelevant, and about 3 used her powers in place of missing anti-air [forum]. The problem was designed in.
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced (the largest forum sample, Browder's stated intent, a Blizzard designer's own experience, two other genres). Two Blizzard points remain second-hand. The Nova quote rests on Player.One alone, since the full Lowko interview with Huck doesn't cover Nova's power level. Browder's "Kerrigan on the battlefield in almost every mission" comes only from preview summaries ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/hands-on-with-starcraft-ii-heart-of-the-swarms-campaig-5806131)) and is not in the full Shacknews interview.
- *Where sources disagree:* About 4–5 posters like an overpowered Kerrigan as the fantasy. See HM-11 for the reconciliation.
- *Limits from play:* a camp's Sentry is safe from the player's automatic targeting only while the heroes hold none, so a second is taken only with care (the AI squad will kill it), and losing yours makes the next easy again. Prefer limits like this, which come from play, over counts or a line of fiction.
- *Sources:* [Battle.net 3998](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/3998), [Battle.net 11624](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/11624), [Battle.net 21065](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/21065), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/68627/interview-starcraft-2-heart-swarm), [BlizzCon 2016 panel 27:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgUXKzgxZ-Y&t=1631s), [Shadow Tactics GDC 08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=508s), [Shadow Tactics postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mimimi-s-i-shadow-tactics-blades-of-the-shogun-i-), [Non-linear levels 25:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CTBor4rhnQs&t=1559s), [Player.One](https://player.one/starcraft-ii-nova-covert-ops-lead-designer-talks-gameplay-terrans-and-tactical-nukes-522696), [Gaming Nexus](https://gamingnexus.com/Article/3052/Warhammer-402c000-Dawn-of-War-II---Retribution-Postmortem-Interview), [TL](https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/402757-heart-of-the-swarm-an-overview-and-review-aa)

## HM-11 Overpowered moments are set-pieces

**Save overpowered-hero moments for one-off set-pieces framed by the story, not the campaign baseline.**

- *Backing:* Templar's Return, a late rotating-hero-pairs mission, drew "absurdly overpowered… and that was glorious" [forum]. The resentment in HotS was about Kerrigan as the *standing* condition of every mission [forum].
- *Evidence:* Thin (one enthusiastic reply versus a large complaint pool).
- *Where sources disagree:* Halo warns that "if the game goes from easy to hard and back again, players attribute the easy parts to poor AI and the hard parts to evil level designers" [dev]. Vary intensity and framing (a story-marked power mission), but keep enemy competence consistent within an enemy type.
- *Sources:* [TL TLCH 17](https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/498211-tlch-mission-17-templars-return), [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/21592), [Halo talk 33:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=76kZtNaetx0&t=2016s)

## HM-12 The squad comes from the script

**Hand out the squad through the script, cap it by type, ration refills, and top it up before set-pieces.**

- *Backing:* Blizzard's "installation missions" give the player "a group of warriors" in "a squad-based system" [dev]. In Supreme, Dehaka grants a capped squad and replaces losses after each boss [wiki]. Ghost of a Chance re-sends lost Ravens and Reapers once [wiki].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Where sources disagree:* Shadow Gambit later let players choose their crew freely [dev]. The suggestion is a designer-set squad by default, with any pick framed as a limited-slot trade-off.
- *Sources:* [LotV panel 45:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKp_7buQ-v8&t=2748s), [Fandom](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme), [Fandom](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Ghost_of_a_Chance), [Shadow Tactics GDC 32:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1945s), [LotV panel 04:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKp_7buQ-v8&t=251s), [Softpedia](https://www.softpedia.com/reviews/games/pc/Dawn-of-War-II-Review-105623.shtml), [Massive](https://www.massive.se/?p=9065), [Meaningful Choice 50:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BEF4GVNzkUw&t=3056s), [GOG](https://www.gog.com/news/shadow_gambit_the_cursed_crew_is_out_read_an_interview_with_games_head_of_design_moritz_wagner)

## HM-13 Choices are informed, tried and correctable

**Make hero loadout and upgrade choices informed, tried and correctable: brief the threats, let the player try both options before an exclusive pick, and allow changes at checkpoints.**

- *Backing:* Browder wanted mid-mission power changes so players never hit "I chose the wrong Kerrigan. Reload", "which is something that we don't want you to have to do". He also wanted no dominant option: "no Internet page you can go to go, 'Well, I'll always choose that'" [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced (Blizzard first-party, plus Relic and Obsidian on cost).
- *Sources:* [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/68627/interview-starcraft-2-heart-swarm), [Huck NCO interview 09:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S1_Gwk7F7k4&t=569s), [BlizzCon 2011 panel 12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LpG32KBYZKI&t=755s), [09:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LpG32KBYZKI&t=563s), [Softpedia](https://www.softpedia.com/reviews/games/pc/Dawn-of-War-II-Review-105623.shtml), [GamesRadar retrospective p.2](https://www.gamesradar.com/how-dawn-of-war-ii-risked-it-all-to-push-a-genre-forward/2/), [Pillars retrospective 26:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0RW3upLoJI&t=1568s)

## HM-14 The heroes alone can always win

**Every mission must be winnable with the guaranteed minimum roster (the two heroes). Squad units and allies widen the options but are never required.**

- *Backing:* CoH3's designers build missions so "they can always be completed" with any company or perks [dev]. Myth's fixed armies could slide into unwinnable states [critic].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced for "winnable with any loadout". Applying it to heroes alone is our inference.
- *Where sources disagree:* God of War makes the companion the vehicle for a level's mechanic: "press square and [he] fires the arrow for you" [dev]. That works because Atreus can't be lost.
- *Sources:* [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/company-of-heroes-3/dynamic-mission-design), [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/strategy/i-wish-bungie-would-stop-designing-excellent-multiplayer-shooters-and-go-back-to-making-fantasy-strategy-games-where-you-blow-up-zombies-with-dwarf-bombs/), [GoW level design talk 21:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=1307s)

## HM-15 Recovery between encounters, as geography

**Put recovery between encounters (full heals, heal stations with visible recharge, or capturable beacons), and keep those spaces safe until the design breaks them on purpose.**

- *Backing:* Ghost of a Chance restores Nova's health "to full between areas". Back in the Saddle's healing stations restore health and energy, some on a visible countdown [wiki]. Founding of Durotar resurrects heroes at Resurrection Stones [wiki].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Sources:* [Fandom: Ghost of a Chance](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Ghost_of_a_Chance), [Fandom: Back in the Saddle](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Back_in_the_Saddle), [warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Founding_of_Durotar)

**Treat reinforcement and healing as geography: a capturable beacon or landing zone the player takes and holds. The next beacon should be visible from the current one.**

- *Backing:* Ground Control 2 delivers reinforcements only to landing zones the player holds [critic]. DoW2's mechanic is now confirmed by a contemporary review: "always have a reinforcement point somewhere near your squads", and bosses push players to retreat "to replenish health" [critic]. Its failure was sameness rather than the formula [critic].
- *Evidence:* Moderate (DoW2 now verified; GC2 is a preview).
- *Where sources disagree:* Retreat to recover is a designed rest in Halo. The recharging shield lets the player learn a tactic is wrong, retreat and return at full health [dev].
- *Sources:* [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/gaming/2012/ground_control_2_pc_multiplayer_impressions/index.html), [Softpedia](https://www.softpedia.com/reviews/games/pc/Dawn-of-War-II-Review-105623.shtml), [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/games/warhammer_40k_dawn_of_war_ii/b/pc/archive/2009/09/22/review), [Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/reviews/warhammer-40000-dawn-of-war-ii-review), [Halo talk 34:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=76kZtNaetx0&t=2081s), [VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/reviews/warhammer-40000-dawn-of-war-ii-retribution-review)

## HM-16 Every pickup is a decision

**Consider health from fighting (kill-drop pickups) to keep a small force aggressive and cut downtime.**

- *Backing:* Diablo III rejected regenerating health for health globes dropped by killed monsters, which keeps downtime low and the player pushing at low health [dev].
- *Evidence:* Sourced for Diablo. Transfer to SC2 is our inference.
- *Sources:* Engadget's report of the WWI 2008 Diablo III design-fundamentals panel

**Give every pickup a decision: consumables the fights genuinely need, placed so that grabbing them means leaving safety.**

- *Backing:* Piercing the Shroud's consumable weapon pickups "tie into the mission" because fights are too strong for the squad alone [forum]. Placing pickups ahead of the player also counters solo-player caution (HM-6). Placing them to pull the player out of safety is our inference [mine].
- *Evidence:* Thin.
- *Sources:* [TL](https://tl.net/forum/single-player/507931-favourite-campaign-missions)

## HM-17 Fail on the pair, checkpoint anyway

**With both heroes present, use a downed state with partner revive and fail the mission only if both go down. With one hero, checkpoint generously. Checkpoint even though free saving exists.**

- *Backing:* SC1 Homeland is hated because "you have 3 Heroes and you have got to keep them alive otherwise you lose", which drove save-reloading [forum]. The campaign revive helpers exist [data]. Mimimi's whole failure model rests on instant quicksave.
- *Evidence:* Moderate for "lean on fast retries". The downed state is our inference.
- *Sources:* [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/21592), [Shadow Tactics GDC 38:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=2322s), [Shadow Tactics postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/postmortem-mimimi-s-i-shadow-tactics-blades-of-the-shogun-i-), [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-shadow-gambit-the-cursed-crew-was-able-to-make-save-scumming-a-central-mechanic), [Elizabeth talk 40:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KKz45K4teqA&t=2417s)

## HM-18 Slow down; never a flaky net

**On detection or ambush, slow the moment down rather than rescue the player. Never demand precise input from a panicking player, and never ship a safety net that sometimes fails.**

- *Backing:* Mimimi spent about a year on a "freeze and teleport" second chance on detection. It failed three ways. Agitated players clicked through it: "If someone is agitated [and] you force them to make a controlled input, that just doesn't work." Players got seen on purpose to teleport somewhere useful.
- *Evidence:* Thin (one first-party account, detailed).
- *Sources:* [Shadow Tactics GDC 35:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=2133s), [36:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=2197s), [37:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=2260s)

**Build a recovery valve against soft-locks: scripted reinforcements at story beats, or relief arriving when the player's strength falls below a threshold.**

- *Backing:* Myth's no-resupply missions could become unwinnable after early losses [critic]. Lydell calls starting large and whittling down "less interesting over time" [dev]. Company of Heroes' Carentan sends reinforcements only when the squad is nearly dead, so desperation turns to relief [ld].
- *Evidence:* Thin to moderate.
- *Sources:* [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/strategy/i-wish-bungie-would-stop-designing-excellent-multiplayer-shooters-and-go-back-to-making-fantasy-strategy-games-where-you-blow-up-zombies-with-dwarf-bombs/), [GamesRadar retrospective p.2](https://www.gamesradar.com/how-dawn-of-war-ii-risked-it-all-to-push-a-genre-forward/2/), [Taylor Part 2](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/ten-principles-of-good-level-design-part-2-)

## HM-19 Difficulty through composition

**Scale difficulty by enemy composition and behaviour, not by hit points. On Brutal, enemies should use cloak, focus heroes and flank allies. Put the hardest content in an opt-in layer.**

- *Backing:* Players call SC2 Brutal "a joke" because the AI doesn't use cloaking [forum]. NCO pack 2 on Brutal won praise as "unforgiving… blown away by the level design" [forum]. Larian doesn't level-scale [dev].
- *Evidence:* Moderate to well-sourced.
- *Sources:* [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/3998), [TL](https://teamliquid.net/forum/single-player/512079-nova-covert-ops-mission-pack-2-release-2016-aug-2), [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/interview/9494-baldur-s-gate-iii-interview-discussing-combat-and-gameplay-with-larian-studios-designer-matt-holland), [Meaningful Choice 37:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BEF4GVNzkUw&t=2248s), [Huck, BlizzCon 2016 interview 13:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lylVQAwZhqM&t=806s), [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/103473/beneath-a-starless-sky-pillars-of-eternity-and-the-infinity-engine-era-of-rpgs?page=27), [TL NCO p.3](https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/512079-nova-covert-ops-mission-pack-2-release-2016-aug-2?page=3), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/68627/interview-starcraft-2-heart-swarm)

## HM-20 One meaningful approach fork

**Offer at least one meaningful approach fork per mission (stealth or loud, two routes, two tools), signal it in the fiction and the briefing, and never gate progress on information.**

- *Backing:* In the Enemy's Shadow is the most-praised single hero mission, with "so many valid approaches" [forum], and its routes depend on Nova's loadout [wiki]. Browder planned Kerrigan styles (sneaky, defensive, air-focused, roach-focused) matched to "the nature of the mission" [dev]. Paths must be named and shown, or players "will think it's linear" [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Where sources disagree:* The TL author who loved Piercing the Shroud called its "switch points" choices "a little gimmicky" [forum]. Forks must change how the next encounter plays, and the unchosen branch must be lost.
- *Sources:* [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/9922), [Fandom](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/In_the_Enemy%27s_Shadow), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/68627/interview-starcraft-2-heart-swarm), [Shadow Tactics GDC 19:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1198s), [Desperados III devcom 46:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2786s), [Hitman talk 08:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=516s), [37:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=2230s), [42:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=2548s), [40:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=2418s), [43:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=2612s), [Meaningful Choice 41:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BEF4GVNzkUw&t=2494s), [Taylor Part 1](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-ten-principles-of-good-level-design-part-1-), [TL](https://tl.net/forum/single-player/507931-favourite-campaign-missions)

## HM-21 Side spaces pay in power

**Put optional side spaces off the critical path but visible from it, make them pay in power rather than score, and never make a missable reward feel mandatory.**

- *Backing:* Supreme's four relics each give a Kerrigan level, Breakout's cell blocks upgrade allied spawns, and In the Enemy's Shadow has gear rooms [wiki]. Taylor #7 puts harder optional content off-path with risk and reward signposted [ld]. IOI puts harder opportunities "further in", behind layers of trespassing [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Where sources disagree:* Hidden gear drove replays [forum], but Browder thinks forcing a replay to collect "would kind of suck" [dev]. Missables should stay optional or be recoverable.
- *Sources:* [Fandom: Supreme](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme), [Fandom: Breakout](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Breakout), [Fandom: In the Enemy's Shadow](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/In_the_Enemy%27s_Shadow), [Taylor Part 2](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/ten-principles-of-good-level-design-part-2-), [Hitman talk 53:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=3199s), [GoW level design talk 64:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=3863s), [51:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=3119s), [Desperados III devcom 48:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2909s), [TL NCO p.5](https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/512079-nova-covert-ops-mission-pack-2-release-2016-aug-2?page=5), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/68627/interview-starcraft-2-heart-swarm), [Fandom: Conviction](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Conviction)

**Cap optional rewards so farming never beats the objective. Prefer rewards that feed the objective, signpost camp difficulty, and don't respawn camps in story missions.**

- *Backing:* In HotS's Hanamura, players found taking camps beat playing the objective, and Blizzard pulled the map. The rework made a camp push lanes instead of dropping a heal [dev]. WC3 RPG-map convention respawns the open world but not dungeons, and reviewers judge maps by camp colour tiers [forum].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced for Hanamura. Thin for the colour-tier and respawn conventions.
- *Sources:* [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/5987/one-year-later-blizzards-second-attempt-at-hanamura-in-hots), [Hive](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/creating-a-new-rpg-a-few-wc3-editor-noob-questions.349434), [Hive review](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/veilswamp.314769/)

## HM-22 Optional challenges teach replays

**Use optional challenges that teach and reward creative replays, score approach and preservation rather than time, and write them so they don't spoil the mechanic.**

- *Backing:* Desperados III's badges "encourage the players to retry missions with different approaches" [dev]. IOI used a list of ten deliberately simple, "revealing" challenges to teach the alert behaviour players hadn't yet seen, and players then "began to set goals for themselves" [dev]. Blizzard ranks co-op by score, not time, "because some missions are variable in length" [dev].
- *Evidence:* Moderate to well-sourced.
- *Where sources disagree:* DoW2's debrief rated kills, force preservation and speed, and a low rating brought both "frustration" and "the determination to play through the game again" [critic]. Leave speed out of the rating or keep it minor.
- *Sources:* [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-the-real-time-stealth-and-combat-of-i-desperados-iii-i-), [Hitman talk 24:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=1460s), [27:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hc8_W2PERZE&t=1653s), [Huck, BlizzCon 2016 interview 08:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lylVQAwZhqM&t=498s), [Battle.net](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/sc2/raw/21592), [TL TLCH 10](https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/498186-tlch-mission-10-the-infinite-cycle), [Softpedia](https://www.softpedia.com/reviews/games/pc/Dawn-of-War-II-Review-105623.shtml)

## HM-23 Art for orientation

**Use level art for orientation and put detail where the player's attention is: one landmark per zone, distinct silhouettes and ground treatment per sub-area, eye-catchers at the next goal, and a unique landmark in every revisited map.**

- *Backing:* Desperados III's environment art serves orientation through landmarks, distinct silhouettes, a different ground treatment per sub-area, a painted path that reveals a hidden door, and eye-catchers of text, movement and colour [dev]. Small stories justify level-design affordances: a broken balustrade "looks like it's getting fixed" where the player must jump down [dev]. Visual appeal "shouldn't come at a cost to the readability" [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Sources:* [Desperados III devcom 11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=700s), [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=762s), [19:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=1143s), [18:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=1081s), [GoW level design talk 26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=1560s), [LotV panel 18:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sKp_7buQ-v8&t=1135s), [BlizzCon 2016 panel 12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgUXKzgxZ-Y&t=744s), [Pillars retrospective 12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0RW3upLoJI&t=756s)

## HM-24 Dialogue: gameplay first

**In-level dialogue runs gameplay first, then character and world, then plot (which goes in cinematics). Place banter in breathers after hard encounters, let the triggering hero speak first, and gate lines on objective progress with a cooldown.**

- *Backing:* Desperados III's priority list puts gameplay support first, then characterisation and world, then plot last, because "you can have the best most revelatory line you've ever written and it does not matter when players are... murdering and killing enemy guards" [dev]. Dialogue goes at bottlenecks and low-enemy stretches right after a hard setup, and arcs conclude before the goal [dev]. Banter tripwires on paths fire the next line on objective progress, then wait a minute [dev].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced (two Mimimi sources plus three others).
- *Sources:* [Desperados III devcom 39:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2348s), [40:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2410s), [42:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2535s), [49:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2972s), [47:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLGBbFPjicQ&t=2848s), [Shadow Tactics GDC 24:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1449s), [26:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1572s), [27:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4h2ZvfYjHp0&t=1634s), [GoW level design talk 18:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eSB29qx6sWw&t=1121s), [Elizabeth talk 22:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KKz45K4teqA&t=1331s), [Pillars retrospective 30:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0RW3upLoJI&t=1817s)

## HM-25 Reuse encounters, never structure

**Reuse encounter templates through a shared library, but build every map new and never repeat a mission's structure.**

- *Backing:* Retribution flipped a Chaos Rising map 180 degrees, recoloured it and passed it off as a jungle, and players reportedly didn't notice [dev]. Critics hammered DoW2 for reusing both maps *and* the loop ("drop in, capture points, kill a boss, leave") [critic]. Softpedia forgave the reuse, because "the situation (terrain, cover or lack of it, available abilities) changes enough" [critic].
- *Evidence:* Well-sourced.
- *Where sources disagree:* Softpedia forgives map reuse, while Game Informer calls the "same handful of maps" the biggest flaw. The strongest complaints follow loop repetition more than map repetition.
