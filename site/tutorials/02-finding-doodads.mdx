You'll find the doodads to dress one set piece in *Ashfall Relay*: the relay at the Relay Station, which the Marine has come to restore. Finding them is a Doodad Search. The set piece is broken into Search Parts, each part gets a shortlist of doodads from the Asset Catalog, and Claude judges every candidate from its picture. You pick from a page of ranked cards. Then you'll browse a second set piece in the Doodad Finder, where you type the asks yourself and tick what you like.

This carries on from [Your first Mission](/tutorials/first-mission/): a Workspace with *Ashfall Relay* in it. Doodads belong to Stage 3, palette and placement, but a Doodad Search can run at any Stage, or for no Mission at all. It stops at the page: turning the picks into a Doodad Composite and placing it is the [`exemplar-design`](/skills/exemplar-design/) skill's work.

The turns below are written by hand. Claude's words will differ in your session, but the tool calls are the ones it makes.

<Objective id="parts" title="Name the set piece and its Search Parts">

Say what the object is, and which Mission it's for. The [`doodad-search`](/skills/doodad-search/) skill takes it from there.

<You>I need doodads for the relay in ashfall-relay: the comms relay at the Relay Station that the Marine comes to restore.</You>

<Claude>
I'll break the relay into Search Parts. Each one says what it has to read as to the player, from the game camera, sized against a Marine. I judge every candidate against that line:

1. **Mast:** a tall comms tower with a dish and antennas, as tall as a Command Center, that reads as a relay the Marine has come to restore.
2. **Power feed:** a generator or heavy cables running into the base of the mast. A machine never stands alone: its supply makes it read as working.
3. **Warning lamp:** a red lamp at the mast's foot that reads as "relay down" until the Marine gets there.

Shall I search for these three?
</Claude>

<You>Yes, those three.</You>

The lines are worth getting right: a vague one ("an antenna") brings back every aerial in the game. Claude suggests parts you missed, such as the power feed here. A Style Pack with interiors or dressing knowledge gives it more of these kits.

</Objective>

<Objective id="by-meaning" title="Narrow each part by meaning" needs="Doodad Finder">

For each part, Claude asks the Doodad Finder's narrowing with the part's line. It finds doodads that share no word with the line, and its ranker, Jev, grades each from its text, from 0 (doesn't fit) to 3 (reads as exactly this).

>

</ToolCall>

<ToolCall name="find_doodads" args={{ ask: "power feed: a generator or heavy cables running into the base of the mast", mission: "ashfall-relay", count: 8 }} />

For the mast, Deadman's Port Tower comes first, with Snow Refinery Terran Antenna, the Purifier Communications Tower, Landing Tower, Antenna and Korhal Antenna close behind, all near level 2, "could work with dressing or scale". For the power feed: the Valhalla Generator first, then Power Cables Warfield and the Moebius Lab Armored Generator, with Space Platform Cables Large and the Korhal Generator Core further down. </Claude>

Jev reads text, never pictures: each ask sends only the ask, its tags and the candidates' text cards. Each ranked ask costs about a tenth of a cent. Without its key, `find_doodads` gives the narrowing's order and says why. [Ranked doodad and set search](/install/#optional-ranked-doodad-and-set-search) says how to give it one.

Without the Doodad Finder package, the server doesn't list `find_doodads`. Claude then narrows every part with `search_doodads`, the next step's keyword search, running a few queries of a few words each.

</Objective>

<Objective id="by-name" title="Find the lamps by their names">

Lights, and some other doodads with no description in the catalog, have only their id words: a search by meaning misses them. Claude finds those with a keyword search, and with no facets, since any facet drops a doodad with no description.

<Claude>
<ToolCall name="search_doodads" args={{ words: ["alarm", "beacon", "lamp"], mission: "ashfall-relay", limit: 8 }}>matches whole words in names, ids and looks</ToolCall>

Three alarms: the Alarm Light, a caged red lamp with a hexagonal floor alarm as its other variation, the Umojan Lab Alarm, which hangs on a wall, and the Star Ship Adun Alarm, which is Protoss gold. The rest are walls and furniture that happen to have lamps.
</Claude>

Words match whole words and their plurals (`crate` finds crates, not crater). End one in `*` to match the start of words: `stamp*` finds stamper. Give `mission` and the doodads the Mission's dependencies already bring in sort first.

</Objective>

Sheets are built from renders, which `npm run thumbnails` makes in an sc2-adjutant checkout, on a machine whose Host Adapter can render models (`model.render`, which the macOS and Windows ones have). Building a sheet from them needs ImageMagick. Without renders, Claude judges from the gallery pictures and says so on each card ("judged from one angle"). A sheet frames each model to fill its tile, so it shows no scale: the gallery picture's Marine does.

<Objective id="finder" title="Browse in the Doodad Finder" needs="Doodad Finder">

The Doodad Finder is the quick way through the parts and the shortlists: a page on your own machine where you type asks and see ranked cards at once, then tick the ones worth a closer look. Use it when you want to browse many asks in a row. Try it on a second set piece, a supply drop on the Landing Pad.

<You>Open the Doodad Finder.</You>

<Claude>
<ToolCall name="open_doodad_finder" args={{}}>checks what it needs, starts it, and opens the page</ToolCall>

The Doodad Finder is open at `http://127.0.0.1:4634/`. Its embeddings model is cached, and Jev has its key, so the cards are ranked. It keeps running after this session ends: ask me to stop it when you're done.
</Claude>

In the Finder:

1. Choose *ashfall-relay* as the Mission, so the cards its dependencies already provide come first.
2. Name the object: **Object**, type `Supply drop`, then **Start**. The tray is now kept as that object's Doodad Search.
3. Type an ask, `supply crates stacked two high on a landing pad`, and tick the cards you like. Then a second ask, `fuel drums`, and tick some of those. Each ask you tick from becomes a Search Part, and its ticks that part's shortlist.
4. Click **Look closer**. It copies a prompt naming the search's `search.json`. Paste it to Claude.
