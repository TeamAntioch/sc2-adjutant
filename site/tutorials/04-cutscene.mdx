You'll add a scene to *Ashfall Relay* and see it played in the game as a Cinematic: a cutscene filmed on sets, with the speakers' faces on screen, cut to their lines. On the bridge of their ship, two Marines watch a broadcast of *Hamlet*. The camera pushes in on the screen and cuts to the play itself: the gravedigger scene, at a bare graveside.

Nobody has recorded the lines, so they get placeholder voices, spoken by your computer's text-to-speech. Placeholders give every line a real length, so the shots are cut to the lines as they'll play. A recording you add later replaces its placeholder, and the next compile uses it.

[`speak_placeholder_voices`](/tools/speak_placeholder_voices/) uses your computer's own text-to-speech, through the Host Adapter: `say` on macOS, and Windows' speech voices on Windows. It's free, runs offline and needs no key. Placeholders are encoded to `.ogg` with ffmpeg, so install that first: `brew install ffmpeg` on a Mac, `winget install BtbN.FFmpeg.GPL.9.0` on Windows. The Campaign Mod gives each character a voice (`mods/example/blueprint.json`, each character's `voice`).
